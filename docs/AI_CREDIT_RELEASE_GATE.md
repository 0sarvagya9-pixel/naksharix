# Paid AI response credits — release gate
One credit is reserved per explicitly chosen paid AI request and consumed only after a naturally completed answer. Free requests never fall back automatically to paid. A failed or expired reservation is released; a response lost after server completion may require support reconciliation (no stored private answer or prompt). A verified partial refund suspends the remaining grant for review. A full refund revokes its remaining credit access; refund decisions remain with the merchant. No wallet top-up, auto-renewal, medical/financial claims or fabricated charges.
