# NAKSHARIX original product illustrations (2026-10-27)

All twelve /public/shop/*-illustration.svg files are original programmatically drawn, vector-scalable catalogue visuals. They are **illustrative art**, not photographs of actual supplied stock, gemstone grades, bead authenticity, certified materials, or licenced third-party product imagery. They are suitable for fast high-resolution responsive card rendering without copying a competitor's pictures.

The public Shop labels illustrative imagery honestly. Admin-authored external HTTPS images still require provenance/evidence validation. Stock quantities are not displayed to customers. Paid checkout and real product authenticity/delivery must be reviewed separately.
