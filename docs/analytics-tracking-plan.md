# Consented aggregate analytics

The deployed first-party collector counts public pageviews and allowlisted interactions after explicit consent. It is not Vercel Web Analytics and does not count unique visitors. No actor ID, email, birth data, private route, query string or arbitrary metadata is recorded. Dynamic public detail URLs are reduced to route categories. DNT and Global Privacy Control suppress collection.

Visitors can allow or decline in the footer privacy preferences. The choice stays in localStorage; no analytics visitor cookie is issued. Data is aggregated per UTC day, route category and event, retained 90 days, and is available only to authorized administrators at `/admin/operations` and `/api/admin/analytics`. CSRF, origin, strict body validation and per-instance rate protection apply to the collector.

Search Console impressions/clicks and Google indexing remain separate provider evidence. Do not present synthetic QA visits as organic growth or customer conversion.

## Optional Google Analytics sales measurement (production-only)
The unsafe unconditional browser tag is removed. Google receives only explicit allowlisted server-to-server Measurement Protocol events with separate opt-in; no third-party Google script on customer pages. The anonymous first-party collector remains unchanged. A random tab-session identifier expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Unknown source values and private URLs never reach Google. Product category and monetary amount are included for checkout and verified digital-report purchase only; no names, contact or birth details, private content, account IDs or raw gateway IDs. Ad data/personalization denied. No GA events from preview/test deployments.
Required production server variables: GA4_MEASUREMENT_ID (public stream ID) and GA4_MEASUREMENT_PROTOCOL_SECRET (secret entered directly in Vercel, never NEXT_PUBLIC, never committed). Do not set legacy NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID. Both values and production runtime required.
Purchase events require Razorpay live key matching, live-order marker, captured/paid state and report purpose; legacy orders lacking proof of live mode are excluded. Stable hashed transaction ID and atomic audit claim prevent duplicate callback reporting. The atomic claim is retained even for failed/unknown transport; no automatic retry after an ambiguous network result. Reconcile against authoritative payment data rather than risk duplicate reporting. Browser consent is checked at checkout and verification; webhook-only captures may not appear in GA, and user-declined events are intentionally excluded. Production DB and Razorpay reconciliation, including refunds and disputes, remain the authoritative 10,000-sale counter. GA transport HTTP 2xx is not proof of ingestion; inspect Realtime/DebugView and later reports before declaring data collection verified. Measurement Protocol-only has limited reporting versus browser tagging; use GSC plus existing first-party aggregates for SEO and funnel health. No synthetic purchases in production.
